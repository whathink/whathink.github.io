Voice

“Read aloud” and the mic button both use your browser's built-in speech features — the voice is generated on your own device and the text is never sent to any voice service. Voice quality depends on your phone/browser; Chrome usually sounds best. (An earlier plan to use an outside voice service was dropped so that your conversations stay only between you and Google.)

Honest limits of this app

  • Chats live in this tab's memory only — close the tab and every chat is cleared. Nothing is uploaded or saved anywhere.
  • No image generation and no web search — answers come only from the AI model (or the Demo Brain).
  • Every person needs their own free Gemini key. Free keys have a daily limit set by Google; when one model is busy, Shaka AI automatically tries the next one.
  • No sign-in, no account, no ads, no payment — ever.
